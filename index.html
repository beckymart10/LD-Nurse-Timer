Safety

This prototype is a personal workflow/reminder aid, not a medical device or substitute for clinical assessment. Presets do not establish the correct monitoring frequency. Requirements can change with maternal/fetal condition, medications, labor stage, orders, and facility policy. Follow current hospital policy, provider orders, and clinical judgment. Never delay assessment or intervention because a timer is not due. Due-Time History records reminder times only; it does not prove an assessment was performed. Verify the actual fetal tracing/EHR and chart according to policy. Do not enter patient identifiers.
